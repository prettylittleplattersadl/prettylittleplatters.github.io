Our story

Meet Nikki & Tamara.

We're Nikki and Tamara” lifelong friends who met in kindy, grew up together, and have shared decades of friendship, laughter, milestones, and memories. As busy mums with a passion for bringing people together, we found ourselves doing what we love most: creating beautiful grazing experiences for family, friends, and special occasions.

What began as a shared love of food, entertaining, and attention to detail quickly grew into a dream of creating something together. Combining Nikki's background in event management with Tamara's natural flair for hosting, Pretty Little Platters ADL was born. ✨🧀🍓🌿

Today, we create thoughtfully styled platters and grazing experiences designed to make every gathering feel a little more special. Whether you're celebrating a milestone, hosting a corporate event, or simply bringing loved ones together, we're here to help create moments worth sharing.

Beautiful food. Meaningful connections. Made with care.
